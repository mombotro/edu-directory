Day 30 - Chap Books

Chap Books:
A chapbook is a small booklet of poems, usually between 10 and 40 pages. The word comes from "chapmen," the traveling sellers who carried small, cheap booklets from town to town. Today many poets still publish their first work as a chapbook. To finish this unit, you will turn your best poems into your own chapbook.

Directions: Choose your 5 to 8 favorite poems from the last 29 days. Revise each one: read it aloud, fix any line that sounds wrong, and check spelling. Then put the poems in an order that feels right, and build your chapbook.
Parts of your chapbook:
1. Cover. A title for the whole collection, your name, and a simple drawing or pattern.
2. Title page. The title, your name, and the year.
3. Dedication. One line, "For ...," to someone who matters.
4. Table of contents. List the poems and page numbers.
5. Poems. One poem per page, with the title above it. Leave space around the words.
6. About the author. Two or three sentences in the third person ("Sam lives in...").
How to order your poems: Start with a strong poem that grabs the reader. End with a poem that feels like a finish. Place poems of the same mood or form so they do not crowd each other, and put short poems between long ones.
How to make the booklet: Print or write each page on a sheet of paper. Stack 4 sheets (each sheet holds 4 pages: 2 on each side), fold them in half together, and staple twice along the fold. This makes a 16-page booklet. Cut or fold the pages evenly. Share your chapbook with someone and ask them to read it.
