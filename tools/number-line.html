Number Line Tool

One line, four ways to use it. Explore — drag the pin and watch the numbers. Jump & Solve — see adding, subtracting, and times tables as hops along the line. Practice — find numbers, read numbers, and measure distances. Print — clean number lines for paper work.

Explore the line
0

Drag the pin, tap anywhere on the line, or use the ◀ ▶ buttons (arrow keys work too when the pin is picked up). Numbers to the right are bigger; numbers to the left are smaller. On the −10 to 10 line, watch the pin cross zero — numbers below zero are smaller than every number above zero.

Jump & Solve
47 + 25 = ?
Press Jump to hop along the line.

Adding hops to the right, subtracting hops to the left. For ×, the first number is how many jumps and the second is how big each jump is — that's the times tables walking along the line. Smart chunks breaks 25 into +20 then +5, the way you'd do it in your head.

In Find the Number, drag the pin (or tap the line) and press Check. In the other two games, type your answer. Hiding the numbers leaves only the ticks — count them carefully!
