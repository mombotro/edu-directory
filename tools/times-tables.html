Times Tables Tool

Learn the tables, drill them, and print them — all in one place. Learn shows each fact as groups of dots and as skip-counting. Practice drills the tables you pick, and any fact you miss comes back again until you get it. Print makes a clean chart or practice sheet.

    Facts to keep practising:

    Each drill asks every fact once — 7 × 8 and 8 × 7 count as the same fact, unless you tick Include turn-around facts. The only repeats are facts you miss: in Practice a missed fact is shown with its answer, then comes back a few questions later until you get it right on your own. In Sprint you have 60 seconds: answer as many as you can, wrong answers just move you on, and your missed facts are listed at the end.
